- Makes the Dutch/Flemish graph less lattice-like by reducing and varying collision clearance, shortening teacher/workshop target distances, and strengthening relationship attraction.
- Tightens connected Dutch/Flemish outliers toward the centroid of their actual first-degree network without name-specific exceptions.
- Raises Dutch/Flemish background-edge opacity from 0.14 to 0.20 (selected edges 0.78) so relationships remain legible at overview scale.
- Includes all v0.20.5 identity-admission fixes. No SQL migration required.
