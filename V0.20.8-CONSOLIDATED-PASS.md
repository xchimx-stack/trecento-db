# v0.20.8 — Consolidated interface, resolver, and layout pass

## Interface
- Warm museum-catalogue ground and restrained network accents.
- Compact network identity at upper left.
- Overview button removed. Methodology moved to the lower-right status line.
- Artist drawer uses editorial serif hierarchy and a single collapse/expand tab; no close X.
- City filter changes preserve the selected artist/drawer. A selected artist remains visible as context even when its own city is filtered out.
- Filter refits ease rather than snapping.
- Chronology guides are slightly more legible.

## Dutch/Flemish
- Stronger visible relationship lines.
- Stronger connected-outlier correction and sparse-node regional cohesion.
- Authority-layout corrections place David Teniers the Younger with Antwerp and Vermeer with Delft.
- Cursor atmosphere reduced to ~4.5px maximum with a click dead-zone.

## Trecento resolver/data
- Resolver audit now includes existing accepted artists missing region or chronology, rather than skipping them.
- Wikidata work location/residence/formation/birth/death place labels feed the region evidence scorer.
- Admission can patch only missing chronology/geography on an already-existing matched artist.
- Year/date pages and malformed leading-comma records are excluded from graph output and future admission.
- One-time SQL cleanup is in `supabase/v0.20.8-cleanup-bad-admissions.sql`.

## Representative images
- Images must occur in substantive Wikipedia article content. Navbox/footer/template thumbnails are ignored.
- If the article body has no eligible image, the drawer intentionally shows `No image`.
