Methodology

Methodology version 0.17.0 · updated with this release

This network is machine-assisted. Artist identities and relationships are normalized from Getty ULAN, Wikipedia, Fondazione Zeri, VIAF, Wikidata, and curated manual overrides.

ULAN is the preferred authority source for artist identity and formal relationship evidence. Wikipedia supplements relationships, chronology, geography, aliases, and media. Zeri is currently retained as an external research link when available; automated connoisseurial extraction is disabled pending a reliable access method. VIAF is used as an identity crosswalk, not as a relationship source.

Automated extraction can produce false matches, omissions, bad geography, duplicate identities, or over-broad relationship classifications. Anonymous masters and proposed identities require stricter authority reconciliation. Manually reviewed overrides can supersede machine-derived values.

The visualization is an exploratory research map, not a catalogue raisonné and not a definitive statement of attribution or influence.
